MELIOREM: An Integrated Evaluation Cyberinfrastructure towards Safe and Dependable Autonomous Driving Systems

About the Project

MELIOREM advances the safety and dependability of autonomous vehicles through efficient, comprehensive testing on high-performance computing infrastructure.

Overall Objective

Develop the MELIOREM testing infrastructure with scalable fuzzing, fault injection, simulation, and safety-case analysis tools to identify vulnerabilities in autonomous driving systems across evolving traffic and environmental conditions.

Long-Term Goal

Enable autonomous vehicle developers, reliability researchers, and domain scientists to efficiently characterize safety vulnerabilities, understand failure behavior, and improve the dependability of autonomous driving systems using practical and scalable testing capabilities.

Project Impact

Support safer autonomous vehicle development by bridging high-performance computing, software reliability, driving simulation, and collision analysis, while providing reusable tools and testing capabilities for safety-critical cyber systems.
